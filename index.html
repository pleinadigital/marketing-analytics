Engagement Rate Channel & per-video (likes + comments + shares) / views × 100 %

Views Channel & per-video Count of qualifying plays in the reporting month. Per YouTube's counting rules (effective March 31, 2025): a Short counts a view the moment the video begins to play; a non-Shorts (long-form) video counts a view only after the viewer watches 30 seconds or more. Count
APV (Avg. Percent Viewed) All videos (avg view duration / video length) × 100. Color tier is length-aware: green = ≥ YouTube's top-10 APV for that video length, amber = ≥ median, red = below median. Benchmarks interpolated from YouTube's published table (3/6/10/15/30-min anchors). %
Hook @5s All videos (viewers still watching at t=5s / viewers at t=0s) × 100. Color tier is working defaults pending KOG-specific calibration — Shorts: ≥90 good / 85-90 ok / <85 weak (anchored on the 80-90% Shorts APV target); long-form: ≥90 good / 80-90 ok / <80 weak. %
Hook @30s Long-form only (— for Shorts) (viewers still watching at t=30s / viewers at t=0s) × 100. Color tier per YouTube's published bands: ≥80 good / 70-80 ok / <70 weak (losing the room before the bumper finishes). %
50% Drop All videos First timestamp where retained audience ≤ 50%, where retained audience at t = (viewers still watching at second t / viewers at t=0) × 100 M:SS
